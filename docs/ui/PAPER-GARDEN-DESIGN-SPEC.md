# Paper Garden — Editorial Botanical

## Product role

Paper Garden is a premium-feeling free birthday template. Its job is to make one portrait and one personal message feel like a preserved keepsake rather than a decorated form.

## Art direction

- Primary direction: editorial botanical.
- Supporting influence: handmade archive; paper, specimen labels and a physical photograph.
- Dreamlike influence is limited to three slow petals and the portrait developing. No perpetual decorative loops.
- Signature: a museum-quality pressed-flower cluster overlaps the portrait like a herbarium composition.

Typography uses Fraunces with its real italic face for display, Sriracha for Vietnamese personalization and DM Sans for editorial utility labels.

## Composition rules

- Desktop uses an editorial split: copy left, portrait composition right.
- Mobile stacks the headline above the portrait while retaining the overlap.
- Decorations may overlap the card but must remain fully contained by their own transparent image bounds.
- Keep at most one hero botanical cluster. Small labels and rules must encode archive/specimen meaning.
- Do not use arbitrary sticker sheets, icon grids, generic gradients or evenly scattered decoration.
- Do not hard-code edition numbers or personal claims that users cannot configure. Editorial microcopy must remain meaningful and reusable.

## Quality bar

The template is not ready if the portrait appears before the scene establishes itself, if an asset is visibly cropped by a square atlas, if motion competes with reading, or if decoration can be removed without changing the story.
