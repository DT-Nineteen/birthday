# Template moodboards

## Pink Celebration

- Palette: soft pink `#FEECEA`, ink `#333333`, accent `#FF7882`, paper `#FFF8E4`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Type: Titan One for celebration, Sriracha for labels, Dancing Script for intimate content.
- Signature: a staged portrait entrance followed by the tactile 3D folding letter.

## Midnight Disco

- Palette: midnight `#100C29`, ultraviolet `#7D5CFF`, hot coral `#FF5D86`, electric cyan `#56E7FF`, mirror white `#EEF1FF`.
- Type: Archivo Black for the poster, Shrikhand for expressive names, DM Mono for ticket details.
- Layout: asymmetric club poster on the left, portrait-as-record orbit on the right.
- Motion: crossing spotlights, equalizer rhythm, chrome glints, orbital stars, ticket reveal.
- Signature: the portrait behaves like a glowing disco record rather than a conventional photo card.

## Paper Garden

- Palette: cotton `#F6EEDF`, paper `#FFFAF0`, leaf `#65765A`, marigold `#E4A92F`, dusty rose `#C87973`, terracotta `#A95743`, ink `#49382F`.
- Type: Fraunces for the keepsake headline, Kalam for handwritten notes, DM Sans for quiet utility copy.
- Layout: scrapbook copy on the left, taped botanical portrait print on the right.
- Motion: drifting petals, swaying pressed flowers, a butterfly float, unfolding paper note.
- Signature: a portrait presented as a physical instant-print inside a pressed-paper garden.

The two new templates deliberately avoid the original 40/60 visual grammar: Disco reads as a performance poster; Garden reads as a handmade keepsake spread.
